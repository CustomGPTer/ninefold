What else Ninefold brings

Six puzzle types in one app, a hint that teaches the technique page by page, an explanation of any mistake, races against the computer or a friend's phone on the same wifi, puzzle codes and QR codes, and reading a newspaper puzzle from a photo, all free.

Where the others do more

Both are on Google Play and update themselves. Sudoku.com and Classic Sudoku have large communities and tournaments with other players online. Ninefold races are against the computer or a friend nearby.
