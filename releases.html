Release notes

What changed in each version of Ninefold. The newest is at the top.

Download the latest version

Version 1.0.0. Android app file, 24.0 MB. Android 8 or later. Free.

Version 1.0.0 22 November 2026Latest

  • First release. 97,000 built-in puzzles, each checked by two separate solvers to have exactly one answer: 70,000 classic puzzles in seven levels from Beginner to Master, plus Mini 6×6, Diagonal, Jigsaw, Killer and 16×16.
  • Smart Hint with seventeen techniques plus two for Killer, explained a page at a time with the answer on the last page; Why is this wrong? for any mistake.
  • Learn: the rules, first steps, the tools, where to start, puzzle types, and a page for every strategy with a worked example and practice puzzles.
  • Daily challenge with a calendar, streaks and monthly bronze, silver and gold trophies.
  • Monthly events with twenty pieces each, uncovering one of twelve pictures.
  • Battle: races against three computer players, and races between two phones on the same wifi.
  • Puzzle codes and QR codes for sharing; type in or photograph a newspaper puzzle, read on the phone.
  • 111 achievements, detailed statistics with charts, analysis and replay of every win.
  • Home-screen widget, optional daily reminder, backup and restore in one file, and an update check.
  • Twelve themes, handwritten notes, paper texture, three mistake rules, hint caps, Zen mode and Lightning mode.

Checking your download

Every version is signed with the same key, so an update installs over the last one and keeps your games. Android refuses an update signed by anyone else.

If you want to check the file itself, its SHA-256 checksum is:

0c7ff54751fd6af02e89fd5ebcaa8bbed13d658abac6820df655e6ad77792e56

Older versions, if any, are on the releases page on GitHub.
