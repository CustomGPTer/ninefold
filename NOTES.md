First release of Ninefold, a free Sudoku app for Android with no adverts and nothing to unlock.

- 97,000 built-in puzzles, each checked by two separate solvers to have exactly one answer: 70,000 classic puzzles in seven levels from Beginner to Master, plus Mini 6×6, Diagonal, Jigsaw, Killer and 16×16.
- Smart Hint explains the next move a page at a time, with the answer on the last page; Why is this wrong? explains any mistake.
- Learn: rules, first steps, puzzle types and seventeen strategies, each with a worked example and practice puzzles.
- Daily challenge with a calendar, streaks and monthly trophies; monthly events that uncover a picture.
- Battle: races against three computer players, or against a friend's phone on the same wifi.
- Share puzzles by code or QR; type in or photograph a newspaper puzzle, read on the phone.
- 111 achievements, detailed statistics, analysis and replay of every win.
- Home-screen widget, optional daily reminder, backup and restore in one file, and an update check.
- Twelve themes, handwritten notes and three mistake rules.

Android 8 or later. SHA-256 of Ninefold.apk: see the release notes page at https://customgpter.github.io/ninefold/releases.html
